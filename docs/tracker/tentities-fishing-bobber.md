## Verified as matching vanilla

- Tick order: ground `life` reset, 1200-tick ground despawn, water height probe, FLYING -> BOBBING transition with `velocity * (0.3, 0.2, 0.3)`, gravity only while airborne/dry, inertia 0.92 (`fishing_bobber.rs:309-443`).
- `checkCollision` ray cast, entity filter, `onHitBlock` velocity shortening, `setHookedEntity` sync (`hook id + 1`) (`fishing_bobber.rs:445-492`).
- `retrieve`: hooked-entity pull (`delta * 0.1`), damage 3 for item / 5 for entity, ground override to 2, loot roll with `luck_bonus + player luck`, per-item launch velocity `delta * 0.1` + `sqrt(sqrt(dist^2)) * 0.08` (`fishing_bobber.rs:124-258`).
- `catchingFish` rates: rain `+1`, sky `-1`, nibble `20..=40`, `timeUntilHooked` `20..=80`, `timeUntilLured` `100..=600 - lure ticks`, triangle `9.188` angle drift, tease chance table, splash/bubble/fishing particles, bite kick from the UUID-seeded synced random (`fishing_bobber.rs:260-270, 517-669`).
- Throw setup `(yaw, pitch, triangle(0.5, 0.0103365) * 3, 0.6/len, clamp ±5)` and `shouldStopFishing` (rod in either hand, distance > 1024) (`fishing_bobber.rs:66-119, 494-509, 700-716`).
- `FishingRodItem` cast/reel, sound volume/pitch `0.4/(r*0.4+0.8)`, item damage application, `ITEM_INTERACT_START/FINISH` (`fishing_rod.rs:26-113`).
