# Chunk lighting: Pumpkin vs decompiled vanilla

Verdict: DIFFERS. Pumpkin implements a complete, convergent sky/block light engine — worldgen flood fill over a 3x3 chunk cache, a persistent sky-cut-height cache, runtime increase/decrease queues and incremental `CLightUpdate` packets — whose per-block propagation math largely matches vanilla.
The material divergences are architectural: no section-level light-state dependency graph (`LayerLightSectionStorage`/`DynamicGraphMinFixedPoint`), runtime sky sources derived from a custom cut-height cache instead of `ChunkSkyLightSources`, unloaded neighbours skipped instead of treated as opaque bedrock, and a budgeted tick-thread drain instead of vanilla's dedicated light thread.
Converged light values look correct on the covered paths; the risk is edge-case/timing parity (section-status transitions, unloaded-border relight, tall empty sections), not the core decay formulas.

## Verified matches

- Opacity floor and decay: `decayed(level, opacity) = level - max(1, opacity)` (`crates/pumpkin-world/src/lighting/mod.rs:27-34`) = vanilla `getOpacity` (`LightEngine.java:78-80`); sky descent preserves 15 through transparent blocks straight down (`mod.rs:36-46`, `engine.rs:249-264`, `runtime.rs:719-725`).
- Block light increase/decrease level math, shape-occlusion skip, back-direction skip and `new_level > 1` enqueue threshold match `BlockLightEngine.java:45-110` (`engine.rs:306-390`, `runtime.rs:399-486`).
- `hasDifferentLightProperties`, `isEmptyShape` and shape occlusion semantics match `LightEngine.java:41-49,67-69,82-86` (`engine.rs:1047-1070`, `occlusion.rs:24-39,91-96`).
- Sky fill sets 15 above the surface section; seed/exclusive column top comes from the `WorldSurface` heightmap and runs after carvers/features (`engine.rs:648-665,666-685,1010-1045`; `value.rs:351-380`) = `SkyLightEngine.java:283-301`, chunk state `Lighting` after `Features` (`chunk_state.rs:160-185`).
- Cross-chunk propagation: 3x3 cache with an 18x18 rim, `Source::step` crosses chunk indices at x/z 0/15; rim stored light is seeded so it can flow inward (`engine.rs:100-196,588-645`; generation radius `chunk_state.rs:152-158`) — equivalent coverage to vanilla's neighbour-section graph.
- Incremental light packets: changed-section mask plus border-chunk fan-out (`crates/pumpkin-core/src/world/mod.rs:2525-2531,2686-2706`) and `light_update_for_sections` (`crates/pumpkin-core/src/net/java/chunk_data/light.rs:31-83`) match vanilla's changed-section filter and `onLightUpdate` callback (`ClientboundLightUpdatePacketData.java:36-53`, `LayerLightSectionStorage.java:263-281`).
- Light packet wire format is version-gated: trust-edges bool for 1.16-1.19.4, BitSet masks and explicit array counts for 1.17+ (`crates/pumpkin-protocol/src/java/client/play/light_update.rs:69-137`) = the vanilla codec shape (`ClientboundLightUpdatePacket.java:13-34`, `ClientboundLightUpdatePacketData.java:19-34`).
- Below-world sky = 0 and above-world sky = 15 defaults (`crates/pumpkin-world/src/lighting/chunk_access.rs:142-159`) match vanilla's light-section padding (`LevelLightEngine.java:121-131`).
