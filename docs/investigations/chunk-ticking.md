# Per-chunk ticking audit: Pumpkin vs decompiled vanilla

Verdict: MATCHES on the core mechanics — per-section random-tick rate, TickPriority/deadline ordering, pending-tick save/load, block-before-fluid drain, active-chunk gating and block-entity gating all follow vanilla.
Six small differences remain (spectator lightning, inhabited-time/stage ordering, priority clamping, overdue-delay NBT, and the Paper-only scheduled-tick budget); no subsystem is wholly missing.
All findings are from read-only grep/bounded reads; no source was edited and no build/test was run.

## Verified matches

- **Random ticks are entity-ticking-chunk only.** Pumpkin samples only chunks in `active_chunks` (simulation distance / forced, spectators excluded unless `spectators_generate_chunks`) at `level.rs:585-631` and `world/mod.rs:776-816,2801-2806`; vanilla iterates `getEntityTickingChunks()` at `ServerChunkCache.java:310-313,324`. No mapper calls.
- **Per-section random-tick rate.** For each section whose `randomly_ticking_mask` bit is set, Pumpkin draws `random_tick_speed.max(0)` positions (`level.rs:576,592-628`) and ticks a block and/or fluid via `has_random_ticks` / `has_random_ticking_fluid` (`world/mod.rs:2891-2961`). Vanilla does `tickSpeed` attempts per `isRandomlyTickingBlocks()` section (`ServerLevel.java:2298-2344`, `2537-2546`). Both give an expected `speed * tickingBlocks/4096` hits per section; Pumpkin picks a uniform position and rejects, vanilla picks from a ticking-block list with 12-bit rejection. The Y coordinate uses different RNG bit slices (`level.rs:608-611` vs `Level.java:2580-2583`), statistically equivalent.
- **Section mask maintenance** on block set/replace, for both random-ticking blocks and fluids: `chunk/mod.rs:606-648,831-851`.
- **randomTickSpeed <= 0** disables random ticks and precipitation but not lightning: `level.rs:576`, `world/mod.rs:3452-3459` vs `ServerLevel.java:2537-2546,2554`.
- **Scheduled tick dedup and queues.** Separate block/fluid schedulers, one queued tick per `(position, type)` via `queued_ticks` (`scheduler.rs:12-25,87,111-117`), matching `LevelChunkTicks.java:21,92-108`. Block ticks drain before fluid ticks (`world/mod.rs:2810-2887`), matching `ServerLevel.java:1739-1740`.
- **Stale scheduled ticks are dropped** when the block/fluid at the position no longer matches (`world/mod.rs:2820-2824,2862-2867`) vs vanilla `tickBlock`/`tickFluid` `state.is(type)` guards (`ServerLevel.java:2868-2873,2907-2911`).
- **Drain ordering.** `drain_containers` merges per-chunk due ticks by comparing heads, then continues draining the current chunk while its next head is <= the next chunk's head (`tick/mod.rs:116-137,186-200`) — this is vanilla `DRAIN_ORDER = (triggerTick, priority, subTickOrder)` and the greedy same-container drain in `ScheduledTick.java:9-17` and `LevelTicks.java:319-338`. Pumpkin's `OrderedTick::cmp` is `(priority, sub_tick_order)` (`tick/mod.rs:108-114`) but deadline is already encoded by the per-step queue slots and append order in `scheduler.rs:50-60`.
- **Pending-tick save/load.** Saved ticks get negative `sub_tick_order` so they precede live ticks, and live ticks are saved in booking order (`scheduler.rs:193-203,207-231`) vs `LevelChunkTicks.java:132-146,149-160`. NBT keys `x/y/z/t/p/i` match `SavedTick.java:26-42` and `tick/mod.rs:140-179`.
- **Ticks in inactive/unloaded chunks.** Deadlines keep advancing in loaded inactive chunks and due ticks fire on reactivation; ticks of unloaded chunks are discarded (`level.rs:633-657`, `scheduler.rs:57-77`) vs `LevelTicks.java:262-317`. Chunks with saved ticks register for ticking on load (`world/mod.rs:837-846`, `level.rs:720-722`).
- **Region cancellation** removes scheduled ticks in an area (`scheduler.rs:119-153`) vs `LevelChunkTicks.removeIf` (`LevelChunkTicks.java:110-120`).
- **Block entity ticking inside chunks.** Only block entities in active chunks are ticked, after entity ticking, with a stale-entity guard and comparator flush (`world/mod.rs:2354-2388`); `BlockEntity::tick` defaults to a no-op (`block/entities/mod.rs:81`) so non-ticking entities do nothing. Vanilla ticks its `blockEntityTickers` list after the entity phase (`Level.java:1781-1836`, `ServerLevel.java:2084-2089`). Parallel vs sequential execution is an implementation difference, not a gameplay rule.
- **Weather and lightning per chunk.** 1-in-100000 lightning chance per tick when raining and thundering, lightning-rod target search radius 128, skeleton-horse trap with difficulty * 0.01 and lightning-rod exclusion, visual-only bolt (`world/mod.rs:225,3452-3454,3609-3657,3719-3732`) vs `ServerLevel.java:2554-2577,2614-2632`. Precipitation at `randomTickSpeed` samples, 1-in-48 chance, with ice/snow/cauldron handling (`world/mod.rs:3455-3557`) vs `ServerLevel.java:2537-2542,2582-2612`.
- **Entity ticking gated to ticking chunks.** Entities are filtered to `active_chunks` and loaded chunks before ticking (`world/mod.rs:2203-2232`), matching the entity-ticking-chunk gating in `ServerChunkCache.java:310-313` and `ServerLevel.java:1092-1101`.
- **Spawners.** Natural spawning and custom spawners run once per tick after the chunk passes (`world/mod.rs:2981-3005`) vs `ServerChunkCache.java:934-966`.
