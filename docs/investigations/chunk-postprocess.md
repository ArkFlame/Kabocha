# Chunk post-processing (ProtoChunk -> LevelChunk): Pumpkin vs vanilla

Does post processing exist: partial.
Pumpkin has no per-section `ProtoChunk.postProcessing` list and no `LevelChunk.postProcessGeneration` pass: the FULL stage only rebuilds sections/heightmaps and copies already-scheduled fluid ticks (`chunk_state.rs:282-376`, `generation_cache.rs:797-801`).
Some vanilla effects are approximated (delay-0 fluid ticks from carvers/structures, lazy block-entity promotion), but the marking pipeline, NBT round-trip, heightmap set and proto-chunk upgrade data are absent.

Verified as matching (or close enough to be intentional):

- Stage order and FULL transition: Pumpkin runs `Lighting -> Spawn -> Full` and calls `upgrade_to_level_chunk` exactly at FULL (`chunk_system/generation_cache.rs:790-801`), mirroring the vanilla status pyramid `SPAWN -> FULL` with the `LevelChunk` swap (`ChunkStatusTasks.java:184-210`).
- Sections and palettes are rebuilt from the proto chunk at upgrade (`chunk_state.rs:228-254`), matching the vanilla `LevelChunk(ServerLevel, ProtoChunk, ...)` constructor taking `protoChunk.getSections()` (`LevelChunk.java:190-197`).
- Heightmaps are primed by scanning the finished block map before FULL (`proto_chunk.rs:614-681`, called at `:1057` for carvers and `:1472` for structures), matching vanilla `Heightmap.primeHeightmaps` at terrain generation (`ChunkStatusTasks.java:128-137`).
- Fluid ticks created during worldgen are not lost: `schedule_fluid_tick` (`proto_chunk.rs:488-495`) with delay 0 is transferred into the level chunk's `ChunkTickScheduler` (`chunk_state.rs:356`) and executed by normal tick stepping (`level.rs:643-660`), an approximation of vanilla's fluid portion of `postProcessGeneration`.
- Pending block-entity NBT from structures is carried into the level chunk's pending map (`chunk_state.rs:334-344`) and can still be created later (`pumpkin-core/src/world/mod.rs:8345-8370`), covering the observable effect of vanilla's eager promotion (`LevelChunk.java:848-852`).
- Light state is carried on upgrade (`chunk_state.rs:325-332`), matching `LevelChunk.java:231` (`setLightCorrect(protoChunk.isLightCorrect())`).
- The proto chunk's custom data / blending data survive the upgrade round trip (`chunk_state.rs:359-366`), covering the data-loss case that `ImposterProtoChunk` avoids in vanilla.
